Publication lags — the context that makes this honest

System operators do not publish on wall-clock time. The protocol treats what was knowable at issue time as a first-class constraint:

  • GB: definitive demand arrives ~21 days late; day-ahead forecast comparisons honor that vintage.
  • DK: industry settlement stabilizes after ~18 days.
  • All Australian regions, Ireland, France, Germany, Belgium, Kazakhstan trade zones: near real-time within hours.
  • Lags are not hyphens on a readme — models' lag features are market-lag-safe (GB uses 4- and 5-week lookbacks only).

Intervals, statistics, limitations

  • Rolling split-conformal (14-anchor calibration, nominal 80/90%). Where coverage dips — cold snaps, holidays — we print the failure (see benchmark card).
  • Diebold–Mariano with Newey-West HAC compares models pairwise; outputs include honest "no significant difference" rows.
  • Weather in backtests is archived reanalysis — a proxy for a perfect forecast; the weather-ablation table quantifies what a real NWP run could add.
  • Kazakhstan units are clearing-trade demand from KOREM (~25–45% of physical load), not physical grid load — separate headline, separate caveats.
