How it works

Data sources & methodology (click to collapse)

Demand: NESO (Great Britain), EirGrid (Ireland All-Island), AEMO (Australia NEM regions), RTE (France), SMARD (Germany), Elia (Belgium), Energinet (Denmark), KOREM (Kazakhstan zones — see caveat). Weather: Open-Meteo archive + NWP forecast. Holidays: holidays package.

Models: seasonal-naive baseline, ridge (honest linear floor), LightGBM (calendar + weather + publication-safe lags), zero-shot Chronos-Bolt-mini, and an inverse-MAE ensemble. Champions are picked per market by the leak-free rolling-origin benchmark and retrained daily.

Intervals: rolling split-conformal (14-anchor calibration, 80/90% nominal). Coverage and its failures are measured per slice in BENCHMARK.md.

Honest limitations: KZ is "centralized-trades clearing demand" of KOREM (~25-45% of physical consumption) — its magnitude and dynamics reflect trade, not grid load as such. GB actuals publish ~21 days late. DK industry-settlement data ~18 days late. Forecast weather = NWP is not perfect; ablation shows where it pays off. Fine-cadence zero-shot models degrade over long AR horizons.

Everything is public: code (MIT) · ingest adapters verified against live APIs · raw snapshots retained · no keys anywhere.
