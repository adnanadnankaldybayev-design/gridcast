Why a public benchmark

System operators publish actual demand and their own outlooks, but nobody public maintains an independent, open, daily comparison of forecasting methods on the same protocol across several markets at once. GridCast is that: the same leak-free rolling-origin backtest drives naive, ridge, LightGBM, zero-shot Chronos and an ensemble — per market, with publication lags respected, and with the failures printed next to the wins.

  • No hidden input drift. Publication lag per operator is built into the protocol (GB ~21 days, DK ~18). Every model sees only what was public at the anchor.
  • Real confidence. Rolling split-conformal bands at 80/90% nominal, with honest undercoverage onto cold snaps and holidays disclosed in the benchmark card.
  • No keys, no secrets. Ingest comes straight from operator open APIs (NESO, EirGrid, AEMO, RTE, SMARD, Elia, Energinet, KOREM) and Open-Meteo weather.
